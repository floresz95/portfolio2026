My long story short: “It’s a kind of magic”

Nearly 10 years ago I started my UX career with a project to figure out how guests would interact with a smart home system on a daily basis. After getting over challenges and projects, I graduated as a junior frontend developer. Why would I want to learn coding? I always imagined development as how magic works in Harry Potter, and I wanted to look under the hood.

Soon I was working with developers at MediaMarkt-Saturn GmbH. A few months passed and I found myself developing and designing an advanced design system in the cybersecurity domain.

The challenge: provide components that are easy to use, safe, and compliant with accessibility standards like WCAG AA. I worked with product designers to support their projects and with design system developers to create components, while also assisting other developers in their daily tasks.

In the meantime I was experimenting with AI tools like Claude and Figma Make. Both impressive.
