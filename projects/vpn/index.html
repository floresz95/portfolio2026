Overview

I played a key role in designing and bringing LastPass’s new VPN application to life. To validate the product, we needed a solid design foundation: a distinct visual identity, design assets, user flows, and a clear UX strategy. Here’s a walkthrough of how I approached these challenges and helped shape the product from concept to release.

  • New stream of income
  • Feature diversification
  • Higher user satisfaction

The LastPass VPN

The problem

To expand our portfolio and better serve our users’ evolving privacy needs, we decided to explore a dedicated VPN solution. Beyond portfolio diversification, several key insights and market factors signaled a strong demand for this addition.

“Would be nice to have a VPN built in my LastPass account”
“I have to register to a different company if I want to browse safely”
“Why don’t you guys have a VPN solution already?”

Research & Discovery

Using the Double Diamond framework, our research combined user interviews with rapid testing to uncover core problems and validate solutions quickly. This approach ensured we stayed grounded in real user needs while efficiently iterating toward a clear, effective product direction.

User interviews

We started with a comprehensive discovery phase, combining user interviews, desk research, sales and support data, community feedback, and product analytics.

The takeaway was clear. Our users needed a reliable, friction-free way to protect their online privacy and browse safely.

Sketching & Ideation

Using data to guide early concepts, I set out to balance simplicity with power.

I started shaping both the brand presence and core product UI, creating concepts for the logo, dynamic background animations, location and smart-routing cards, state management, and the main application layout.

Design Process

Double Diamond… we all know how it works, and how sometimes it doesn’t. Instead, let me show you what I have been working on and what the final designs are.

The logo

I needed to design an icon and identity that reflected our core mission: creating a safe, private online environment for our users. Above all, clarity was essential. The app icon had to be simple and instantly recognizable so users wouldn’t waste time searching for it on a busy desktop or screen.

Actually, a long road led to the final solution.

The background animation

To highlight our global server coverage, I designed an abstract representation of the world rather than a literal globe. LastPass VPN is a serious, feature-packed security app, so I used this visual approach to inject warmth into the UI and make the overall experience feel much friendlier.

The Country cards

I designed the Country cards with speed and clarity in mind. The cards needed to be hard to miss and conveniently placed, displaying just enough information for users to make a quick decision. By keeping the content focused only on what was essential, I kept the connection experience simple and stress-free.

The Smart location card

Finding the optimal server shouldn’t take effort. I was focused on creating the absolute simplest path for our users, which led to the Smart Location card. It acts as a primary 1-click gateway to toggle the VPN on and off. I kept the visual states extremely clear, switching between an idle state with a “Connect” button and an active state with “Disconnect”, so users always know their connection status at a glance.

The Connect – Disconnect state flow

When handling sensitive user data, immediate and transparent system feedback is crucial. Users should never have to guess whether they are secure, connecting, or disconnected. To make the connection state unmistakable at a glance, I established a clear visual status system using distinct color coding, dynamic iconography, and well-timed animations.

All locations screens

While quick options on the main screen are great, users still need an easy way to browse the full server list. To avoid overstimulating the main dashboard, I introduced a separate location menu complete with a prominent search bar at the top. This kept the main UI clean and distraction-free while giving users full control to find and switch locations in seconds.

The layout

Designing the layout from a blank screen was a major challenge. I started by sketching and prototyping different layouts to test what felt natural and what didn’t. By backing every iteration with research data and user feedback, I shaped a layout that felt right and was validated by our findings. This helped us align on an MVP direction quickly and present a clear proposal to stakeholders.

Impact & Results

Ultimately, my goal was to give our users peace of mind when browsing online. By designing an intuitive experience focused on privacy and security, we created a tool users can rely on every day. LastPass VPN has been a huge win for us: not only does it broaden our product offering and keep our user base engaged, but it also creates a valuable new revenue stream for the business.
